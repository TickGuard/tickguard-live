Understanding Your Tick Risk Score

The TickGuard Risk Score ranges from 0.00 (lowest risk) to 1.00 (highest risk). Each score falls into one of three risk levels:

Low Risk (0.00 – 0.32)

Tick activity is minimal. Outdoor exposure is generally safe, but basic precautions are still recommended.

Medium Risk (0.33 – 0.65)

Tick presence is moderate. Protective clothing, repellents, and post‑activity tick checks are strongly advised.

High Risk (0.66 – 1.00)

Tick activity is high. Avoid tall grass and wooded areas, use repellents, and perform thorough tick checks.
